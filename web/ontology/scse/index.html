HRAI-GO: Human-centred Regulatory AI, Governance Observatory

Shadow Council Simulation Environment Ontology

https://hrai-go.aeonbridge.io/ontology/scse

SCSE module of HRAI-GO (Human-centred Regulatory AI, Governance Observatory): deliberation, council, strata, seats, rounds, positions, scenarios and decisions. Imports the regulatory core instead of redeclaring its classes (ESP-ONT-02, RF-17). Includes synthetic illustration individuals and safe positive SWRL rules. No legal certification or population representativeness is implied.

1.1.0: own namespace; imports the core; regulatory classes (LegalProvision, NormativeRequirement, LegalStatus, LegalException, Jurisdiction, ComplianceAssessment, PolicyAlternative, PolicyObjective) now come from the core; deliberation classes have their home here, with owl:equivalentClass to the legacy core classes kept for compatibility.

1.2.0 (ESP-ONT-02, RF-03): DecisionRecord, position revision and alternative positions, stratum of a stakeholder; rules R29a/R29b (synthetic support never counts as human) and R30 (divergent alternative).
